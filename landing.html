Good to know.

Short answers to what people ask first.

Does it work offline?

The voice downloads the first time you use it. After that, reading runs on your device without sending your files anywhere.

Which files can I use?

PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown and HTML. Scanned PDFs without selectable text can't be read yet.

How do links work?

Web pages are fetched through the free r.jina.ai reader, because browsers can't read other sites directly. Files and pasted text never leave your device.

What does it cost?

Meraify is free to try. Create an account only if you want to sync your library.
